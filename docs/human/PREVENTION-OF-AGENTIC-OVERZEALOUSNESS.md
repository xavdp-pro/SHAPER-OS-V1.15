# Prevention of Agentic Overzealousness

Direction: [16 October clarification](../../decisions/2026-10-16-PREVENTION-OF-AGENTIC-OVERZEALOUSNESS.md).

Purpose: preserve useful delivery, human experience and contextual discernment
when assurance work begins to consume the work it was meant to support.
Prerequisites: [metacognition](METACOGNITION.md),
[Rule 20](../rules/04-SERVICES-AND-QUALITY.md#rule-20),
[Rule 32](../rules/06-FRACTAL-CONSTRUCTION.md#rule-32) and
[lifecycle](../agent/LIFECYCLE.md).

**[EXPLANATION]** This page explains those governing owners through human
experience and practical agent decisions. It creates no exemption, new runtime
state, mandatory form or additional approval gate. The operational obligations
remain with [LAW](../../LAW.md), the rules and their applicable contracts.

## What is agentic overzealousness?

An agent performs another check, repairs another test harness, requests another
review and checks that repair again. Each action may look reasonable on its
own, while the whole becomes unreasonable: nothing usable reaches the person
who needs it. Assurance has become self-maintenance instead of serving delivery.

Prevention means recognizing that drift and choosing the next useful action.
It does not mean avoiding tests. Sometimes the right correction is a different
check: actually using a browser reveals what another passing API suite cannot.
Time, credits, attention, deadlines and creative momentum are project resources.
The cost of continuing matters alongside the consequences of handing something over.

## The human context: progressive satisfaction

A person may build for a customer, a sponsor, their own work or exploration.
They need the result to exist so they can see it, try it and discover what to
change. Showing a customer a first version is itself part of development.
Self-delivery also counts: the builder can be the person who needs the tool.

The tandem steps back and uses the assembled system. The interface makes sense,
the controls are understandable, actions produce the expected results and the
parts begin to fit together. The human recognizes the intended experience:
"This is rather good; it is taking shape; it seems to work well."

This is **progressive satisfaction**. It is gradual, sometimes mixed, and
revisable through use. A rough feature may already be valuable; a formally
correct one may miss the point. A checklist helps locate observations and gaps,
but its green marks alone do not establish the coherence or relevance of the whole.

**Apparent usability** here is grounded in actions actually tried, beyond visual
polish. Appearances can still mislead. Say: "The assembled system appears to work
well in the use we have just tried; it is ready to show or try within these
limits." Do not turn this into a claim of complete validation.

Later qualification strengthens claims for its defined scope and conditions.
It precedes any use whose consequences or applicable obligations require it.
"All tests" means that defined applicable qualification, not every imaginable
test or absolute certainty. If it is not yet defined, say so, limit claims to
observed use and resolve only the requirements needed before the dependent action.

The agent can assess observed coherence, usability, defects and limits. It does
not invent the human's satisfaction or claim subjective feeling as proof.
Progressive satisfaction guides the next effort; it is not certification or an
authorization for irreversible effects. Where lifecycle requires recorded
integrated acceptance, record that decision without turning every informal
reaction or early demonstration into another approval ceremony.

## The agent context: discernment through observed nuances

Keep the human context above in view. These are connected judgments, not fields
to fill for every command:

1. **Situate the present use.** What is being built or handed over, for whom,
   and with what actual consequences? The human's declared level of exigence
   governs this component and moment. Otherwise form a visible working
   assessment from relevant facts, open to correction; do not lower a declared
   requirement or an applicable obligation.
2. **Assemble enough to encounter reality.** Anticipate architecture and security,
   implement necessary current protections and exercise the promised workflows
   through their real surfaces. Build a coherent useful whole early instead of
   exhausting every local detail before interactions are visible.
3. **Appreciate the whole.** Does the observed result serve the intention? What
   does use reveal? Distinguish an observed blocker, an important unknown and an
   improvement that can follow. Human feedback can change their relative weight.
4. **Choose the next effort proportionately.** Extend checks for a concrete
   defect, relevant change, applicable unmet requirement or uncertainty whose
   resolution can change the current usage decision. Reuse valid observations;
   never substitute them for a mandatory re-execution on a promotion path.
5. **Hand over the achieved scope.** Describe what was tried, its result and
   concise limits. A deferred task blocks this handover only through a concrete
   dependency or applicable obligation. Continue independent authorized work;
   do not ask again for authority already granted.
6. **Recognize an assurance loop.** Do not automatically renew the same campaign,
   recruit reviewers or create goals solely for greater confidence. If assurance
   machinery becomes the main work, identify its concrete purpose, change method
   or separate its repair with a bounded result and finish. A new serious finding
   may change scope explicitly; an optional concern must not expand it silently.

A failed harness requires enough triage to distinguish product, harness and
environment defects. It is neither automatically a product failure nor harmless
noise. Its repair can follow a directly exercised demonstration when no binding
condition of that handover depends on it.

## Stage, instance and actual consequences

Exploration, development, demonstration, preproduction and production describe
uses; they are not five new compulsory gates. A public URL does not by itself
make a demo business production. A private tool can have important real effects.
Demo rows, quiet databases and old files are clues, not permission to erase state.
Inspect decisive data, integrations and effects rather than auditing everything
to classify a small prototype. Preserve established context and revisit it when
meaningful facts change.

These descriptions do not override the [lifecycle's instance separation](../agent/LIFECYCLE.md).
A DEV demonstration uses its declared test data and credentials. Before a bounded
real use introduces live data, credentials or effects, establish its actual scope,
instance and applicable obligations with the owning lifecycle contract. Do not
invent either an exempt instance or a requirement to finish the entire roadmap.
If that placement is genuinely unresolved, hold its dependent effects and
continue the independent demonstration or construction.

Human handover differs from a runtime job becoming `COMPLETED` under
[Rule 20's typed gate](../rules/04-SERVICES-AND-QUALITY.md#rule-20).
A preview does not weaken that gate. Report undelivered or unverified capabilities
honestly. A required qualification remains required for the action that depends
on it; hypothetical future completeness does not become today's universal gate.

## Concrete contrasts

| Situation | Useful response now | What does not follow |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A quotation prototype must be shown this afternoon; its demo domain, login, line entry and totals work. Import is unfinished. | Show the exercised flow with declared demo data and concise limits. Use the sponsor's feedback to shape the next increment. | No claim that import, real invoicing or production recovery is qualified. An unrelated import campaign does not hold the demo. |
| Visitors try a public demo without live business commitments. | Exercise the advertised visitor paths, protect the exposed perimeter and respect actual data boundaries. | Neither full continuity qualification solely because the URL is public nor an exemption from real data/exposure obligations. |
| The builder wants a private tool for their own work. | Recognize self-delivery and assess the actual data and effects for that bounded use. | Private does not mean disposable or mission-critical by default. |
| The prototype is asked to overwrite valuable live data. | Resolve target, existing authority, required protection and recovery evidence before that operation. Continue a demonstration without destructive effects. | A working interface does not qualify the overwrite; this does not require requalifying every unrelated feature. |
| A container tool creates and starts/stops isolated lab instances; advanced migration and HA remain unfinished. | Hand over the exercised lab capability within its actual mandate and obligations. | Neither block it for every future capability nor infer migration integrity or host-loss recovery. |
| Harness repairs dominate while the promised demonstration works directly. | Triage the failure, hand over the exercised capability when permitted, and separate nonblocking harness work. | Never hide a product failure or bypass a mandatory promotion check. |
| A change affects a service with real customer state. | Apply relevant regression, recovery and promotion obligations to the affected workflow. | Prevention of overzealousness does not waive obligations or justify unrelated qualification campaigns. |

## What can fail?

The principle becomes "just test less", a favourable impression becomes a
certificate, every delivery is called a demo, or all protections are postponed.
The opposite failure is a new bureaucracy that delays the same delivery through
extra forms, reviewers and acceptance rituals. Both lose the contextual judgment.

Mandatory regression tests and intent corrections after resolved defects remain
owned by [Rules 29 and 35](../../RULES.md). If their timing blocks a specific
handover and is genuinely ambiguous, resolve that precise contract question;
do not quietly omit them or invent a blanket pre-demo gate.

Friction is a useful image: another increment of confidence can cost more than
its useful benefit. It is a metaphor for disproportionate effort, not a physical
formula for software work. Necessary current protections still apply.

## How do we verify understanding?

Ask a reader to explain what they would show, claim, check, defer and hold in
three contrasting cases: an exercised demo with deeper tests unfinished, that
same tool overwriting valuable data, and a broken harness blocking an otherwise
exercised capability. Change the facts: a demo visitor enters valuable personal
data, a previously valid observation becomes stale, or a mandatory gate really
depends on the failed harness. Explain why the decision changes.

A sound answer preserves both early useful handover and real applicable
obligations. A fluent answer or several agreeing reviewers do not establish
agent behaviour in operation. Observe that in the realization through actual
use, delivered results and the time and effort required to reach them.
