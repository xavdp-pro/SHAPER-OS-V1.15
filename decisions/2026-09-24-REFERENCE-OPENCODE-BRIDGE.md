# Operator direction — OpenCode bridge in the reference universe

Status: **[MANDATE]** scoped direction for the proposed reference-universe
composition, given in the 24 September 2026 human–agent conversation. It does
not seal V1.15 as successor runtime law or certify a built image.

The operator selected the OpenCode Cognition Bridge as a **default component of
the reference universe**. This supersedes the earlier 24 September description
of that particular reference as four units without a bridge. The reference
therefore contains Vault, Logger, Queue, Maestro and the OpenCode bridge, each
with its declared contract and, under the September container/MariaDB profile,
its own runtime and private database.

The four foundational responsibilities remain independent of cognition. A
bridge installed by default is not itself a business agent, an authorization
to run arbitrary work, or a reason for Vault, Logger, Queue or Maestro to stop
when an external cognition engine is unavailable. Projects may add their own
agents and business functions after deriving a universe.

This is a scoped reference-composition choice, not a universal requirement
that every possible future SHAPER realization use OpenCode. Existing deployed
universes do not change by this document; their governing revisions and actual
compositions remain separate evidence.
