# Prevention of Agentic Overzealousness

**[MANDATE] Scope:** human-directed clarification of delivery, verification and
stage-dependent exigence in SHAPER OS V1.15, adopted 16 October 2026.

Prevent self-perpetuating assurance work that delays a coherent useful result.
Keep the human experience and the agent's functional judgment connected:
progressive satisfaction through actual use can guide early handover, without
claiming comprehensive qualification or waiving applicable obligations.

The current usage and its consequences determine the appropriate effort within
the human mandate. A public demo, personal use and a live customer service need
not have identical requirements. Preserve the governing lifecycle's data and
instance boundaries. Qualify the actual delivered perimeter and its necessary
dependencies; do not make every future feature a prerequisite for that delivery.

When the current level of exigence is undeclared, the agent forms a visible,
correctable assessment from relevant facts, resolves decisive uncertainty and
continues authorized independent work. A deferred task blocks handover only
through a concrete dependency or applicable obligation. Time, credits, attention
and deadlines are part of the outcome to protect.

or intent corrections, or authorization for external effects. It introduces no
universal test quota, readiness percentage, extra state machine or repeated
approval requirement. Documentary publication is not a runtime qualification.
