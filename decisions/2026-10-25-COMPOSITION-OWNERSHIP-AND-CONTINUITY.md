# Composition, ownership and continuity direction — 25 October 2026

Authority: explicit human direction for the current SHAPER documentary alignment.
Scope: the current V1.15 mental corpus and its reference construction profile.
This direction changes the clauses identified below; earlier decisions retain
their dates and original meaning. Projects record the adopted source revision
and affected scope under [LAW](../LAW.md#two-uses-and-explicit-adoption).
A source update does not itself migrate an instance or change its pinned artifacts.

## The enduring intention

The human–AI tandem steers intention, judgment and useful action across the
whole system. Agents find exact owning sources by task, follow dependency and
referrer links, and reconstruct the complete frame when directing construction.
The ecosystem composition and glossary are independent source owners; SHAPER OS
owns the development and operational laws projected into that composition.

Infrastructure hosts components (functional units, or organelles). The generic
stem cell provides a reusable base; a specialized cell/service combines that
base with one or more domain components. Logical spaces organize services and
relationships. Fractal governance assigns scoped governors, makers and workers
under the tandem. OS, Runtime and Workspace describe responsibility across
these scales. Logical membership, authority and physical placement are distinct.

## Source relationships and evolution

[Composition](../docs/architecture/COMPOSITION.md) owns this edition's projection;
[Source roles](../docs/architecture/SOURCE-ROLES.md) owns its navigation and custody
contract. They link the [ecosystem](https://github.com/xavdp-pro/shaper-ecosystem)
and [independent glossary](https://github.com/xavdp-pro/shaper-glossary).
Original rule IDs and compatibility identifiers are preserved. Future renames
carry explicit old-to-new mappings for links, imports, submodules, images and
identifiers; renaming prose never renames a running artifact.

The selected MariaDB foundation serves stable operations, predictable logs and
maintenance, consistent backup/restore, and small independently recoverable
state owners. Resilience is composed at service and infrastructure level with
bounded failure domains and deliberate promotion of a replacement writer.
The architectural intention is maintained affirmatively in product sources;
source comparisons, capability observations and release records remain in the
private project lab. Licensing and publication remain separate authorized work.
